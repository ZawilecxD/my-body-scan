# 11x-product-refine


Load references `interview` and `product-factory-policy` from sibling `11x-references`. Require shaping notes and research synthesis; missing inputs point to their producing skill. Do not write product code.

## Steps

1. Read `shape-notes.md`, `product-research/synthesis.md` and its evidence. Preserve previous decisions unless new evidence materially challenges them.
2. Present the meaningful findings, including evidence gaps. Ask focused product questions with a recommendation: audience, core journey, differentiator, scope, exclusions, success measures and unresolved risks. Never silently choose a consequential product fork.
3. Record each decision, user answer, rationale, evidence links and remaining uncertainty in `context/foundation/product-decisions.md`. Clearly separate proposed from settled decisions. Update shaping notes to reflect the agreed direction, preserving useful history.
4. Ensure MVP capabilities have verifiable acceptance criteria and that research limitations are either resolved or explicitly accepted as assumptions. Do not label scope settled while consequential questions remain open.

## Done when

The agreed scope is ready for `11x-prd`. Print that command and stop when standalone. Product agreement is not stack approval or build approval.
